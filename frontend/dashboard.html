Accuracy check: –

Every number is labelled: measured comes from a meter, estimate from a rating, and anything not on a meter is not shown. Between two readings the units counter moves by an estimate and snaps to the real value at the next reading. “Charging finished” is an estimate from the power pattern; the laptop’s battery level is not read. Projections are projections, not measured results. Tariff values are examples: replace them with the numbers on your bill.
