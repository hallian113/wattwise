Everything your home’s energy needs

One platform that senses, understands, decides, acts and measures — in kilowatt-hours and Pakistani Rupees.

Real-time monitoring

PZEM-004T meters report power and energy per room and appliance; ESP32 nodes stream them over MQTT.

Occupancy & context

LD2410 mmWave, PIR, light, temperature and door sensors tell WattWise whether a room is occupied, vacant or uncertain.

Tariff-aware analytics

Configurable slabs, protected/unprotected category, peak hours and a month-end bill estimate with 150 / 180 / 190 / 200-unit alerts.

AI & Random Forest

Explainable rules first; Random Forest forecasts of load and occupancy on top — labelled honestly until trained on real data.

Smart automation

Occupancy lighting, smart fan, energy-saving mode and weather safety — manual override always wins.

Waste detection

Finds lights, fans, TVs and chargers left running in vacant rooms and shows the rupee impact.

Weather-aware safety

Weather advisories and voltage deviation raise a precaution status with an occupancy-aware policy. An aid only: it never replaces breakers or surge protection.

What-if planning

Try usage changes and peak-hour shifts and compare labelled estimates of energy and rupee cost side by side.

Sustainability readiness

Efficiency indicators, CO₂e estimates and a net-zero readiness checklist. Solar and storage are prepared for, not claimed.

How it works

A continuous loop that turns raw readings into decisions you can see and override.

1

Sense

ESP32 nodes in each room read presence, climate, light, door state and energy.

2

Understand

The backend combines readings with time, tariff and history to classify each room and appliance.

3

Decide

Explainable rules judge if each load is needed; ML forecasts supply predictions.

4

Act

Recommend or switch supported appliances through relays, infrared or a contactor.

5

Measure

Energy, cost, peak share and projected units are shown on the dashboard.

About WattWise

AI-Driven Context-Aware Energy Optimization and Intelligent Management System — a Final Year Project at COMSATS University Islamabad, Abbottabad Campus.

Prototype scope

  • Three rooms: Lounge, Bedroom, Bathroom
  • ESP32-S3 gateway + ESP32-C3 room nodes over MQTT
  • PZEM-004T, LD2410, PIR, BME280, BH1750, reed switch, relays
  • FastAPI · PostgreSQL · Scikit-learn (Random Forest)
  • Demo Mode for presentations, Live Mode for real hardware — never mixed
